🛶 Raft Ride — Glitch Database

🎮 About the Game
Raft Ride is a Roblox game centered around riding a raft through a large slide course. The player controls a raft as it travels through different sections of the course, with turns, drops, jumps, and other obstacles along the way.
The game gives the player several movement controls that become important when navigating the course. The raft can be directed using the Turn mechanic, while W/Boost can increase its movement speed. There is also a Flip mechanic that allows the raft to flip during a run.
One important part of the game's movement system is that some controls can continue working even when the raft leaves the normal course. This creates unusual interactions between the raft, the slide, and the game's physics.

🛶 The Raft
The raft is the player's main vehicle throughout the game. Most of the run takes place on the intended slide track, but the raft's movement system allows players to control it in unusual situations as well.
The main controls involved in advanced movement are:

↔️ Turn — Changes the direction the raft is facing.

💨 W / Boost — Gives the raft additional movement and speed.

🔄 Flip — Causes the raft to perform a flip.

🛤️ Slide / Track — The main course the raft is supposed to travel through.

🧩 Why Glitch Techniques Are Possible
Raft Ride's movement system can produce unexpected behavior when different controls are used together or when the raft is in unusual positions.
For example, the raft can sometimes remain controllable after leaving the normal track. This means that falling off the course does not always mean the run has to end immediately.
The combination of Turn, Boost, Flip, raft positioning, and the slide's physical geometry creates the basis for the game's advanced glitch techniques.
